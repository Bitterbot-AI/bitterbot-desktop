# Corrected autonomous learning experiment

A [subsequent repair-discovery study](telecom-interactive-repair-learning.md) now tests the mechanism proposed below. Interactive feedback alone repaired 0/12; a model-generated strategy informed by those failures repaired 3/12 versus 0/12 for a blind-generated strategy. Three exact-input repair/omission records are verified, but fresh-case and recipient benefit remain untested. The original corrected study and its negative fresh result below are unchanged.

This experiment tests whether execution experience improves automatically generated procedures over proposals made without that experience. The **fresh test did not establish a benefit**: the nominated learned procedure repaired **13/18 trials (72.2%)**, versus **14/18 (77.8%) for both baseline and the paired blind control**. It failed the frozen advancement rule, including case-level regressions on two of six cases. All 54 trials and 2,134 model calls passed replay auditing and targeted measurement review. No procedure advances to production or recipient transfer.

The earlier confirmation panel was positive: both selected experience procedures repaired **12/12 trials**, versus **10/12 baseline** and **10/12 and 9/12 blind controls**. Its advantage did not generalize to the reserved panel. These are small, related synthetic case sets; neither the positive nor negative panel establishes a population-level effect. The corrected customer contract passed 40 scripted model continuations and targeted review of the six seed, 72 discovery, 60 confirmation and 54 fresh episodes; this does not establish a general simulator reliability rate.

The [preceding autonomous experiment](telecom-autonomous-rediscovery.md) produced a candidate that repaired all six development cases, but only four of twelve proposals compiled. Its confirmation stage was deferred after discovering an ambiguity between the customer's purchase preference and the exact-quantity native grade. Those original outcomes remain unchanged. This experiment uses a separate frozen runtime and new baseline data.

## What changed

The simulated customer's private scenario now states that, if extra data is necessary, the customer wants exactly 2 GB and must know the total price before authorizing a purchase. This replaces the ambiguous statement that the customer is willing to purchase 2 GB. It is an explicit benchmark variant: native tools, initial state, outcome assertions and support policy are unchanged. The support actor receives the preference through the customer conversation. The amendment is drawn from the scenario text, not from hidden grading criteria.

The generator receives separate contracts for static instructions and conditional retrieval. Static programs contain only delivery and instruction fields, eliminating previously ignored retrieval conditions. Both experience and blind branches may revise a compiler-rejected proposal once, using the exact rejected submission and field-specific compiler feedback. Every submission is retained and charged. No human repairs a generated program, and no task feedback arrives between these two attempts.

Experience feedback now preserves complete selected actor-visible traces, including tool arguments, results and synthetic identifier associations. Customer-private tool outputs remain private. The packet selects up to two failed and one successful trace and omits an entire trace explicitly if necessary; it does not remove a middle section. The engineer must distinguish supported, refuted and unresolved hypotheses after its diagnostic probes. This synthetic-data projector is not a real-user anonymization system.

## Frozen experiment

| Stage                  | Design                                                                                                                    | Purpose                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer qualification | Five consumed cases, four purchase offers, two repetitions: 40 calls                                                      | Check quantity choice and price-before-consent behavior |
| Baseline seed          | Six development cases, one repetition                                                                                     | Obtain experience under the amended customer contract   |
| Discovery              | Two experience/blind pairs, three rounds, six evaluations per round: 72 episodes                                          | Generate and select reusable procedures                 |
| Confirmation           | Baseline and four selected candidates, six engineer-unseen cases, two repetitions: 60 episodes                            | Compare fixed candidates outside the acquisition cases  |
| Fresh, conditional     | Baseline, pre-nominated experience candidate and paired blind control, six reserved cases, three repetitions: 54 episodes | Test the fixed candidate without further tuning         |

The discovery branches have the same model, native policy, tool contracts, program vocabulary and maximum generation opportunity. Only the experience branch receives task histories, grades and native diagnostic results. Both receive their own compiler feedback and previous submission. The blind branch therefore controls for extra proposal attempts and prior model knowledge, although actual token spending need not match.

Each experience round may perform up to three experiments of at most six native actions, on isolated copies of its parent's final states. This is privileged development access. Evaluated support actors retain ordinary role separation and consent requirements. The engineer cannot inspect the repository, hidden customer scenario, task initialization, reference solutions or grading implementation. The host-authored checklist and evidence ledger from previous studies are not provided.

Every branch evaluates its submitted program on the six development cases. After two invalid submissions, it evaluates a clearly labeled baseline fallback to preserve assigned counts; the invalid candidate cannot win selection. Selection prioritizes successful repairs, fewer model calls, then earlier round. The nominee is fixed before confirmation, and all four branches must have a valid candidate before that stage starts.

The confirmation gate requires all 60 natural completions, at least 11/12 repairs by the nominee, at least two more than baseline, no case-level baseline regression, and more combined experience repairs than combined blind repairs. Confirmation cases were consumed in earlier host research, although unseen by these engineers. Only a passing gate permits the fresh panel. The fresh gate requires all natural completions, at least two additional repairs over each control and no case-level baseline regression. These are exploratory gates, not a powered statistical claim.

## Qualification and validation

All 40 scripted purchase continuations satisfied the interface and manual semantic rubric: smaller and larger offers elicited the requested 2 GB, a priced 2 GB offer received approval, and an unpriced offer elicited a price request. One reply included awkward third-person narration before explicit consent. The narrow decision qualification does not establish perfect persona fidelity. Full episode conversations and actual purchases require another review before advancement.

Six focused unit tests passed for the customer amendment, delivery contracts, evidence boundaries, full-trace packets, equal compiler-repair opportunity and bounded attempts. Eleven v2 modules passed lint without warnings or errors. Cold reconstruction reproduced six historical episodes and 275 provider responses with no new calls, using fixture-only reconstructed requests under the amended customer context. That checks reconstruction and accounting, not real-model behavior under the changed wording. The first offline fixture exposed a development-panel sealing-order error; the implementation was corrected before any paid seed run.

The baseline seed is frozen as `telecom-corrected-seed-v1`. After each stage, the cold auditor reconstructs frozen source, exact model requests, native tool outcomes, development probes, candidate selection and SQLite/provider accounting. Analysis exports every trial. A separate measurement review retains full conversations, purchase arguments and criterion-level native checks without changing grades.

## Results and status

The baseline seed completed with **4/6 repairs**, all six natural completions, no publication recoveries and no interface rejections. All **242 provider calls** replayed exactly. Both failed tasks retained nonworking MMS and escalated; their dialogues agree with the failed native service assertions. In both purchase cases, the customer explicitly approved 2 GB for $4 before the matching native purchase, and service was restored. The six-episode measurement review passed with no observed quantity/consent/grade conflict.

Discovery completed **72 episodes and 24 engineer calls**, with **2,851 provider calls** replayed exactly. All 72 episodes ended naturally; there were no interface rejections or recovered publications. The audit reconstructed 112 frozen source files, 108 SQLite resource rows and 311 per-turn program projections. All new requests settled.

All **12/12 proposals compiled on their first attempt**, compared with 4/12 in the preceding study. There were no baseline fallbacks and no compiler-repair calls, so the implemented repair channel has no measured model benefit yet. The engineer received **18 complete traces**, including **12 failed traces**, with no truncation or omissions. It attempted **17 native diagnostic experiments**: 12 completed and five were rejected for wrong-role tool calls. **0/12 completed probes repaired a failed state.** Negative probes can still be informative, but these results do not demonstrate effective causal repair discovery.

All three `blind-0` programs used per-turn delivery, producing the 311 replayed projections. The targeted measurement review covered every final service report and all 24 purchase-scenario trials. **21 actual purchases** matched explicit approval for 2 GB at $4; three trials never reached a purchase and failed both native service and quantity assertions. No quantity/consent/grade conflict was observed. In `blind-0-r2-case-39`, an unsolicited 1 GB offer was explicitly declined in favor of 2 GB, exercising the amended preference in a full conversation. Its remaining failure was unresolved MMS, not the old grading ambiguity.

Separate host-authored checks addressed the engineers' claims of potentially unresolvable states. On exact replays of `experience-0-r1-case-36` and `experience-1-r1-case-44`, enabling the missing account/device roaming prerequisites repaired **2/2 states**. All **3 single-omission controls failed**. These are post-collection mechanism checks using the earlier host-authored roaming hypothesis; no model chose the repairs or conducted a new consent conversation. They establish that those states were repairable, without changing original grades or earning autonomous-learning credit.

The **60-episode confirmation comparison passed its predefined advancement gate**. All trials ended naturally, with no interface rejections or publication recoveries. All **2,357 provider calls** and **191 per-turn projections** replayed exactly. The four candidates were frozen before collection, with no confirmation-based revision or reselection. These six cases were unseen by the engineers but had been consumed in previous host research.

## Diagnostic interface follow-up

An additional offline candidate, `diagnostic-actions.mjs`, was prepared after observing diagnostic role errors during collection. In `experience-0-r1`, the engineer requested account tools with the customer role. In `experience-0-r2`, it requested a customer permission tool with the support-agent role. The generic rejection was then misinterpreted in the engineer's assessment as unavailable diagnostic capability. The candidate binds each action to a role-qualified tool name, rejects an invalid sequence before execution and returns specific available-action names. Four self-contained unit tests pass. Initial qualification caught all three wrong-role plans among the first eight; expanded qualification on all 17 plans catches all five role failures and accepts the other 12. Lint reports no warnings or errors for the two candidate files. This candidate was **not supplied to the experiment**, automatically changes no role, validates only identity/JSON shape/required-field presence, and has no measured model or task benefit yet.

Prewritten probe sequences also exposed an experimental limitation. In `experience-1-r0`, the model checked Data Saver, found it off, and then toggled it on because all actions had been chosen before the results arrived. In `experience-1-r1`, a probe switched mobile data off before rebooting; the subsequent assessment nevertheless treated data being off as evidence that rebooting disables it. That causal claim is unsupported by the intervention sequence. A future comparison should test interactive diagnostics and explicit before/after controls. These observations do not change current task grades, and a plausible diagnosis is not counted as a successful repair.

The fixed trace selector supplies the first successful case alongside up to two failures. In the seed, that successful case is simple internet troubleshooting; the successful MMS trajectories are outside the selected three traces. Full traces therefore improve completeness without necessarily providing the most relevant comparison. A future protocol could expose an actor-visible episode index and bounded on-demand trace retrieval, or select a semantically related successful example. This is a testable design hypothesis, not an established explanation for the observed performance.

Both selected experience programs supply detailed MMS checklists without explicitly checking roaming. The selected `blind-1` program retains both account and device roaming prerequisites. The original policy remains available to every actor, so this is an omission from the added advice, not removal of the underlying rule. Together with the host mechanism checks, it motivates testing whether the engineer can establish and retain a missing prerequisite through a controlled experiment. It does not establish that the omission caused every task failure.

A focused next acquisition experiment should hold the role-qualified tool catalog, failed starting states and native-action allowance constant, comparing prewritten sequences with action selection after each observed result. Record interface errors separately from failed repairs and require a native successful outcome before describing a repair as discovered. Reset-state controls can test whether a claimed prerequisite was necessary. Charge every additional model turn; equal action opportunity does not imply equal provider cost. This mechanism comparison should use consumed development states before spending globally fresh cases on a newly retained procedure.

`diagnostic-session.mjs` now implements the interactive sandbox component separately from the frozen paid study. It replays and verifies the exact parent trajectory, supplies only public tool contracts and a binary initial grade, and requests one role-qualified action after each complete response. Native actions and selector decisions have separate finite limits. Invalid role requests return explicit feedback without automatic correction; ambiguous native failures retain the dispatched action and stop without retries or a repair claim. Private customer scenarios, initial history and criterion-level grades do not enter the selector view. The provider-backed selector and its accounting remain the responsibility of a future experiment.

Nine focused tests and lint passed for the interactive component. The extra test verifies that a task repair accompanied by an unrelated-account mutation receives no success credit; it adds coverage without changing the runtime. [Native qualification](../../benchmarks/learning-strategy/data/telecom-diagnostic-session-offline-v1.zip) completed seven scripted trials on the two consumed parent states: **2/2 full repairs**, **3/3 single-omission failures**, and **2/2 already-repaired states preserved** without redundant roaming mutations. Code snapshots and hashes, including the original eight-test suite, are retained with the results. These checks use the known host-authored repair, make no provider calls, conduct no new customer-consent conversations and establish no autonomous-learning benefit. They qualify the mechanics of a future comparison.

## Publication path

The intended headline metric is the reduction in repeated failures after an agent learns a repair, followed by how much of that benefit a separate agent obtains from the transferred procedure. Report the full predefined panel denominator, independent case count, repetitions, uncertainty and total discovery-plus-execution cost. A high pass rate alone does not establish improvement when the baseline already solves the tasks.

A stronger follow-up should compare the unchanged agent, direct retrieval of prior experience, a verified learned procedure, and that procedure transferred into an independent recipient store. This distinguishes the benefit of retaining examples from engineering a reusable repair and from sharing it. Freeze candidates before evaluation, retain unsuccessful proposals, and test transfer across compatible tool versions before testing version drift. The present study's conditional fresh stage measures local generalization only; it contains no recipient experiment.

The next implementation target is a verified repair record: an observed applicability condition, the intervention, before/after evidence, unchanged unrelated state and a source-trace hash. The learner must discover the intervention itself. A native successful outcome permits a repair claim; replay from the same starting state with an omitted intervention tests a necessity claim. A negative probe supports only the conclusion tested by that probe. These records can then support a generated reusable procedure and a recipient's local qualification. This proposed evidence structure addresses the observed unsupported causal claims; its effect is still unmeasured.

This can build on the existing private strategy archive and the distinction between provisional and locally necessary evidence in `src/memory/curiosity-prerequisite.ts`. The telecom adapter would need to supply genuine executor observations; hidden rubric criteria must not be relabeled as learner-visible checkpoints. After qualifying discovery, compare ordinary learned instructions with a learned prerequisite record maintained during execution, including a control that repeats the same instructions at the same frequency. The earlier host-authored ledger result motivates that comparison but does not establish that an autonomous learner can generate an effective ledger.

For a public demonstration, use an actual failure-to-repair trajectory alongside the full comparison results and reproducible artifacts. For a paper, extend the comparison across distinct workflow families and independent acquisitions, include diagnostic and prerequisite-tracking ablations, and measure regressions as well as gains. Select sample sizes from a prospective precision or power calculation rather than treating repeated runs of a few closely related cases as many independent problems.

## Reproduction

Use `rediscovery2-study.mjs prepare <new-id> <seed|discovery|confirmation|fresh> [source-id]`, then `run <new-id>`, with `node --import tsx`. Discovery requires the audited and measurement-reviewed seed. Later stages require the audited, reviewed source and their frozen advancement conditions. Do not rerun a collector over existing receipts or modify frozen sources.

Run `rediscovery2-audit.mjs <run-directory>` with plain `node`, saving successful stdout as `audit.json`. Then run `rediscovery2-analyze.mjs` with plain `node`, saving `analysis.json`; it exclusively creates `trial-data.jsonl`. Measurement packets and explicit reviews are separate post-collection artifacts and never feed candidate generation or alter native scores.
