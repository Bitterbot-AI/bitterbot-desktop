# Further experimentation

1. **Repeat on the release runtime.** Freeze model, image, tool exposure and pricing; test fresh authored instances with the same paired schedule. Confirm skill discovery, helper use and interruption accounting after the runtime port. Keep historical and new results separate.
2. **Measure delayed retention.** Run untouched retention fixtures in fresh sessions after at least 24 hours, recording the same package hash and environment. No retention result is included in this release. Do not turn an elapsed deadline into an observation.
3. **Distinguish executable reuse from other advantages.** Compare baseline, instructions only, the executable package, an unrelated helper, and a strong hand-written/cached workflow. Keep total acquisition, repair, validation and execution ceilings equal. Record whether the model actually uses each intervention.
4. **Replicate acquisition.** Train several independent helpers from different failure histories across document, spreadsheet, data-cleaning and API workflows. Partition acquisition, selection and final evaluation by task family and source corpus. Use independent physical recipient nodes and unseen users, not just fresh IDs or document genres.
5. **Test the collective contribution directly.** Compare exchanged executable packages against the same exchange plus permitted pooled evidence. Introduce conditional failures in distinct environments, retain contradictions, and trace proposed repairs to their evidence. Peer reports may prioritize investigation but cannot confer local qualification.
6. **Qualify useful endpoints before the study.** Correctness remains mandatory, but efficiency should be a distinct planned endpoint: no practically meaningful degradation under a declared margin, then lower all-in cost or latency. Do not require the baseline to be perfect. Predeclare treatment of task failures, interruptions, invalid checkers and unknown costs. Any production efficiency-promotion path requires its own reviewed design and tests; this release retains the existing success-improvement gate.
7. **Power the primary comparison.** Use a separate development pilot to estimate variance and freeze sample size for 80% power at a five-percentage-point success improvement. Analyze paired family-clustered uncertainty and independent training replicas. If the budget cannot support the sample, report feasibility rather than efficacy.
8. **Check human value and safety.** Add blinded usefulness and document-layout ratings, authorization/privacy regression panels, revocation during work, model/tool drift, canary rollback and adversarial evidence. Track learning, failed proposals, retries, verification, transfers, retention and unresolved charges. Publish negative and inconclusive outcomes unchanged.

All future paid work remains subject to the existing budget and specific study authorization. This release preparation initiates no new model experiment and grants no automatic publication or adoption authority.
